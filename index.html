About Our App

Tired of waiting at the bus stop wondering when your ride will show up? BitBus is your ultimate public bus transit companion, designed to take the guesswork out of your daily commute. Whether you are a daily professional, a student, or a weekend explorer, BitBus puts the entire Bitola city’s public bus transit network right in your pocket.

At-a-Glance Info

Essential data like arrival times and bus stop locations are front and center, so you spend less time looking at your screen and more time moving.

Interactive Transit Map

Visualize your journey. Locate nearby bus stops. Click any bus stop and see exactly how many minutes until the next buses arrive.

Comprehensive Schedules

Plan ahead with ease. Access full timetables for every bus line in Bitola city to organize your day with confidence.

Smart Bus Stop Search

Quickly find bus stop locations and see which lines service them, ensuring you always take the most efficient path.

User-Friendly Interface

Designed for simplicity BitBus features a clean, intuitive interface that eliminates clutter, allowing you to find your bus line or stop in just a couple of taps.

Free to Use

BitBus is accessible for everyone. Enjoy full access to your bus schedules, maps, and arrival times without ever hitting a paywall or needing a subscription.
